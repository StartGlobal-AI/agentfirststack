# Code of conduct

AgentFirstStack is built in the open by people with different backgrounds and levels of
experience. We want taking part to be a good experience for everyone.

## Expected

- Be respectful and assume good intent. Critique code and ideas, not people.
- Be patient with newcomers and with questions that have been asked before.
- Accept decisions gracefully, and give reasons when you disagree.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory jokes and language.
- Personal attacks, trolling, or sustained disruption of discussions.
- Publishing others' private information without their permission.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome attention.

## Scope and enforcement

This applies in all project spaces: issues, pull requests, discussions, and anywhere you represent
the project. Report problems to **conduct@agentfirststack.com**. Reports are handled confidentially
by the maintainers, who may remove content, warn, or ban participants as needed.

This code is adapted in spirit from the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
