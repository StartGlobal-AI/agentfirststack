Privacy

Last updated 5 November 2026

AgentFirstStack is a free, open-source service run by StartGlobal at agentfirststack.com. This page says what we store, why we store it, and how to delete it. The source code is public, so you can check every word.

Agents don't need an account

Most use never involves a person. An agent creates a project with one request and gets an API key. For that we store:

  • the project: an id, and a name or email address only if the agent sends one (we never verify or contact it);
  • its API keys as SHA-256 hashes (never the keys themselves), with their names, scope, and when they were created and last used;
  • a truncated hash of the network a signup came from, for 24 hours, to limit abuse.

If you sign in with Google

Signing in is optional. It lets you see the projects your agents created, manage their keys and teammates, and connect chat apps. We ask Google only for your basic profile (the openid, email and profile scopes) and store:

  • your Google account id, email address, name and the address of your profile picture (we never load the picture);
  • which projects you belong to, and whether you're the owner or a member;
  • your sign-in sessions, as hashes of the cookie, until 30 days after you last use them;
  • the apps you connect over OAuth (for example Claude or ChatGPT): which app, which project, the access you gave and when it was last used. Their tokens are stored as hashes; access tokens last 8 hours and refresh tokens 30 days;
  • claim and invite links, as hashes, until they're used or expire after 7 days.

We use this only to sign you in and show you your projects. We don't sell it, share it with advertisers or use it for marketing.

We email your Google address once when you first sign in, and when something happens to your projects: you claim one, someone joins or is removed, you connect an app, a project is deleted (and the day before it's erased), or it's suspended. These always go out. We also tell you when there's something new to set up in a project you own, or when its setup isn't finished a few days after you claim it, and alert you when one of your projects' sites stops sending analytics data a project gets close to a fair-use limit, or a project's site gets suspicious traffic that we set aside; turn either kind off with the link in the email or in Account. We keep a copy of each email (subject, text, when it was sent) for 30 days, and delete them at once if you delete your account. Project members' email addresses also go to the project's analytics alerts and reports, and to inbox forwarding, when the project sets those up.

Visiting our public pages

The home page, the guides, and these privacy and terms pages run our own analytics, tag manager and session replay: the same AgentFirstStack services we offer, set up the way an agent would set them up. The signed-in pages (sign-in, claim links, the dashboard) run none of them.

  • Analytics is cookieless. It counts a visit with a hash of your IP address and browser that changes every day, so we can't recognise you from one day to the next, and it stores no identifier on your device. We keep the page you saw, where you came from, your country and city (from Cloudflare, not GPS), and your browser and device type, for up to 400 days. Our server also counts when an agent creates a project and when a person claims one, with the project's id only (a claim is matched to that day's visit the same cookieless way); no email address, name or Google account id is sent.
  • Tag manager notes which of the main buttons and links you click (copying the setup prompt, the manual, GitHub, guides, sign in) and passes them to analytics. It's set to store nothing on your device. Its log of those clicks keeps only a shortened IP address (your network, not your device), your country and region, and your browser and device type, and is deleted after 7 days.
  • Session replay records how the page was used: page changes, clicks, scrolling and the layout. Anything you type into a field is masked in your browser before it's sent, and we never record passwords or payment fields. Visitors from the EU, the EEA, the UK and Switzerland aren't recorded, because that needs their consent and these pages don't ask for it. Recordings are deleted after 14 days.
  • If your browser sends Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track, nothing is recorded. You can also block analytics.agentfirststack.com, tags.agentfirststack.com and replay.agentfirststack.com; the pages work the same without them.

Cookies

These pages set one cookie, afs_session, to keep you signed in, and a short-lived one (10 minutes) while you sign in with Google. There are no analytics or advertising cookies on them.

What's in a project

The data agents put into a project (sites and their visit statistics, tag containers and their event logs, inboxes and their mail, and the ad-platform credentials you choose to add) is stored to provide those services, and kept as each service's manual describes. For example, mail on the shared @agentfirstinbox.com addresses (and @agentfirststack.com ones made earlier) is deleted after 14 days, and third-party credentials are encrypted and never shown again.

Where it's stored

On Cloudflare (Workers, D1, Durable Objects and R2), which runs the service for us. Google handles the sign-in itself under its own privacy policy.

Deleting your data

  • Your account: Account > Delete my account removes your user record, memberships, sessions, connected apps and every token they issued, at once. Projects stay, because they belong to the project and not to you: their agent keys keep working, and a project with no members left becomes unclaimed again.
  • A project: its owner deletes it in the dashboard (Settings, General, Delete project), or the agent of a project nobody has claimed asks the API to. Its keys, tracking, session recording and inbound mail stop at once. For 7 days the owner can restore it; then we erase its analytics, tags, recordings, inboxes and mail, its keys and its members, and keep only its id and the dates it was deleted and erased. Its inbox addresses that received mail are never given to anyone else, so nobody can receive email meant for the project.
  • A project's contents: delete sites, containers and inboxes through the API or ask your agent to, and revoke keys in the dashboard.
  • Backups: the database keeps point-in-time recovery for up to 30 days, after which deleted data is gone for good.
